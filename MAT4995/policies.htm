Computation of Grades

The evaluation of students will be done according to the following work that needs to be completed throughout semester:

  • A research report, which summarizes recent works in a direction on quantum computing with connections to algebra. The report should be about 15 pages long, but longer reports are also accepted. The report should explain the results of 3 or more closely related research papers, evaluate the merits of these results, and propose concrete research problems that stem from the reviewed literature.
  • A short presentation (30 to 40 minutes) half-way through the semester on the research report. The presentation should be in the form of a lecture for a working seminar, and therefore it should be accessible to an audience that is not expert in the subfield of the report (as opposed to a research-level seminar that is given to an expert audience). Clarity and accessibility of the presentation are important factors in the evaluation.
  • A long presentation (60 minutes) near the end of the semester on the research report. The criteria for evaluation of the long presentation are the same as those used for the short presentation.
  • Typesetting lecture notes of 2 to 3 lectures with LaTeX.

Report 50%
Short presentation: 20%
Long presentation: 20%
Typeset notes: 10%
