Official Course Syllabus

Rings and ideals, homomorphisms and isomorphism theorems, principal ideal domains and factorial rings, polynomial rings and the construction of finite fields. Modules, direct sums and annihilators of modules, free modules, classification of modules over a PID.

Prerequisites

MAT 2141, MAT 2143.

Course Texts

There is no mandatory textbook, as the lecture notes will be provided by the instructor electronically. The following open-source textbooks are supplementary references for the course, and contains several exercises for your practice. They can be downloaded free of charge from the URL's below.
