How to succeed in the final exam:

  • The final exam is based on all of the material that was covered in class throughout the semester. The Syllabus webpage contains the details of the sections and topics covered.
  • You should know all of the definitions that were mentioned in class. As a reference, you can use the class notes that have been uploaded on Virtual Campus. In the exam, if you are asked to write a definition, you should write the definition as precisely as possible. This does not mean that your definition should be word for word the same as the one given in the notes. It means that your definition should be accurate. If you state a definition vaguely, you will lose points.
  • You should review the assignments. Study the posted solutions and compare them to your own solutions. After you are done with reviewing the assignments, it is a very good idea to work on the questions recommended For Further Practice which are posted on the Syllabus page. Although some of them are probably too hard to be on the exam, they will test your knowledge and your ablility to use the techniques you have learned in this course.
  • You should know the statements of all of the theorems that were mentioned in class. Again, you can use the uploaded notes as a reference. Note that some theorems have titles, e.g., the Rational Roots Theorem or the Multiplication Theorem or the Chinese Remainder Theorem. You should know the titles of these theorems.
  • In the exam, solving some of the questions may require application of some theorems. Whenever you are using a theorem to solve a question, you should clearly indicate which theorem you are using (e.g., by mentioning its title, or briefly describing the statement of the theorem).
  • In the exam, you will be asked to prove two theorems. You should know the proofs of the following theorems:

    § 4.3 : Theorem 3.
    § 5.1 : Theorem 5.
    § 5.2 : Theorem 3, Theorem 4.
    § 6.2 : Theorem 1, Theorem 5.
    § 6.4 : Theorem 4.

    The numbering of the theorems is according to the 3rd Edition. If you have purchased an older edition, you should check it with someone in class who has the latest edition.

Good luck!
