Official Course Syllabus

Arithmetic modulo n, permutations, groups, cyclic groups, cosets and Largange's theorem, normal subgroups, homomorphisms, quotient groups, isomorphism theorems, direct products and the structure theorem of finitely generated abelian groups.

Prerequisites

The prerequisite for this course is MAT1341, and one of MAT1348, MAT 1362, or MAT2362.
