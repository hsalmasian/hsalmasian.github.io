Official Course Syllabus

Analysis of cryptographic methods used in authentication and data protection, with particular attention to the underlying mathematics and methods of proof. Selected emerging topics of research relating to post-quantum cryptography.

Prerequisites

MAT 3166 or MAT 2143 and 12 university course units in mathematics (MAT) at the 3000 level or above (or permission of the instructor).

Textbook

The course will mainly be based on the following text:

  • Jeffrey Hoffstein, Jill Pipher, and Joseph H. Silverman, An Introduction to Mathematical Cryptography, 2nd Edition, Undergraduate Texts in Mathematics, Springer, New York, 2014.
