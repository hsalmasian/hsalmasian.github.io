Official Course Syllabus

Review of propositional logic, informal set theory and proofs techniques. Paradoxes, introduction to axiomatic set theory and the encoding of mathematics. Ordinals, ordinal arithmetic. Axiom of choice, well-ordering principle and Zorn’s lemma. Cardinals and cardinality of sets, Cantor-Schr&oulm;der-Bernstein theorem, Cantor's diagonal argument, continuum hypothesis. Introduction to recursive functions and recursive sets.

Prerequisites

MAT 1348 or MAT 1362.
