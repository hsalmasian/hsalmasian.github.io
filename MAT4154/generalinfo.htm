official Course Description

Brief overview of commutative algebra, Hilbert's Nullstellensatz, algebraic sets, and Zariski topology. Affine and projective varieties over algebraically closed fields. Regular functions and rational maps. Additional topics chosen from: the relation of varieties over complex numbers to complex analytic manifolds, genus, divisors, line bundles, Riemann-Roch Theorem, Bézout's Theorem.

Prerequisites

MAT3143

Course Text

The lectures are essentially based on the following text:

  • Perrin, D., Algebraic Geometry: An Introduction, Springer, 2008.

This book is the main text for students enrolled in either MAT4142 or MAT5143. You can download it from the publisher's website through the university proxies.

A more comprehensive textbook, which you can find in the Morisset library, is the following:

  • Hartshorne, R., Algebraic Geometry, Springer, 1977.

You will not need this advanced book. It is merely for students who are interested to learn the subject beyond the course syllabus.
