How to succeed in the midterm exam:

  • The midterm exam is based on all of the material that was covered in class from Chapters 1 through 8 of the textbook.
  • You should know all of the definitions that are mentioned in class. As a reference, you can use the class notes that have been uploaded on Virtual Campus. In the exam, if you are asked to write a definition, you should write the definition as precisely as possible. Your answer need not necessarily be word for word the same as the one given in the notes. However, your answer should be accurate. If you state a definition vaguely, you will lose points.
  • In the exam, you may either directly or indirectly be asked to give examples and non-examples. When you study the definitions, think of examples (e.g., a semisimple Lie algebra, or a derivation) and non-examples (e.g., an example of a solvable Lie algebra which is not nilpotent, or an example of a representation which is indecomposable but not irreducible). The examples mentioned in class and in the assignments will also be helpful.
  • You should review the assignments. Study the posted solutions and compare them to your own solutions. After you are done with reviewing the assignments, it is a very good idea to work on the Recommended Exercises For Further Practice which are posted on the Syllabus webpage. Although some of them are probably too hard to be on the exam, they will test your knowledge and your ablility to use the techniques you have learned in this course.
  • You should know the statements of all of the theorems that are mentioned in class. Again, you can use the uploaded notes as a reference. In the exam, solving some of the questions may require application of some theorems. Whenever you are using a theorem to solve a question, you should clearly indicate which theorem you are using (e.g., by briefly mentioning the statement of the theorem).
  • Write all of the necessary details of your proofs. For example, if you need to use induction, do not simply write "it follows by induction". You should explain the details of the induction argument.
  • In the exam, you will be asked to prove two theorems. You should know the proofs of the following theorems:

    Lemma 4.3
    Lemma 4.4
    Corollary 4.5
    Lemma 5.4
    Proposition 6.2
    Lemma 7.13
    Theorem 8.1
    Theorem 8.2
    Lemma 8.4
    Corollary 8.6

Good luck!
