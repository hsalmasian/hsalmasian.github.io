Course Description

Quantized enveloping algebras are an important family of associative algebras with a product structure that depends on a deformaion parameter q. These algebras were initially discovered in physics (in the study of quantum integrable systems), but they now have a life of their own within mathematics, connecting algebra, geometry, and combinatorics. This course will be a gentle introduction to the vast subject of quantized enveloping algebras.

This course will assume minimal background; in particular, knowledge of Lie algebras will not be required.

Prerequisites

MAT3141 or MAT3143.

Course Texts
